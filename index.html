Project 01

Regulatory Knowledge Platform

Turning fragmented regulatory texts into structured, explorable knowledge.

Problem

Official regulatory material is authoritative, but fragmented across documents, articles, recitals and amendments.

Approach

I’m building a knowledge layer around official European sources, document structure, metadata and legal relationships.

Key design choices

Structured documents · article and recital navigation · linked legal sources · traceability

Explore platform ↗ GitHub ↗

Project 02

Regulatory RAG

Evidence-grounded regulatory question answering.

Question→Plan→Claims→Retrieve→Evidence→Answer
Problem

Simple semantic search breaks down when a regulatory question contains multiple claims or requires evidence from several sources.

Approach

Decompose the question, retrieve evidence for each claim, then constrain generation to the material that was actually found.

Key design choices

Hybrid retrieval · claim-level evidence · citation-bound answers · transparent failure

Demo in progress GitHub profile ↗

Project 03

Second Line Risk Copilot

Turning regulatory obligations into structured ICT risk workflows.

Obligation→Risk→Control→Metric→Challenge→Assessment
Problem

Regulatory obligations rarely arrive in the shape of a usable control, metric or assessment workflow.

Approach

An experiment in bringing regulatory evidence, risk reasoning and structured second-line assessment into one workflow.

Key design choices

Evidence-linked reasoning · control objectives · KPI / KRI · human review and challenge
