Broad Housing · Commercial project

Cube Cloud

Enterprise Platform Engineering

An enterprise platform spanning CRM, ERP, supply chain, warehousing, logistics and business workflows. My work covered system architecture, technology selection, engineering practice and technical delivery.

Overview

The platform brought together systems for customer relationships, enterprise planning, supply chain operations, warehousing and logistics. Business processes crossed those boundaries, so integration and delivery were central parts of the engineering work.

My role combined architecture and technology selection with improving engineering practices, guiding the development team and coordinating delivery. It required attention to both individual services and the way the wider platform was built and operated.

Systems and tools

Business areas
CRM, ERP, supply chain, warehousing, logistics and business workflows.
Services and data
Java, Spring Boot and Spring Cloud; Nacos for service discovery, MySQL and Redis for data, RocketMQ for messaging.
Delivery and operations
Docker, Jenkins / CI/CD, ELK and SkyWalking.

My role

System architecture & technology choices
Contributed to architecture and technology selection for an enterprise platform built around Java microservices and interconnected business systems.
Engineering practice
Worked on improving how the team developed and delivered software, with containerisation, CI/CD and operational tooling as part of the platform’s engineering stack.
Technical leadership
Combined hands-on engineering work with technical management of the development team and coordination of delivery across business requirements and system dependencies.

Engineering context

This experience is the enterprise engineering background behind my current AI projects: service boundaries, integration contracts, operational visibility and the practical work of getting software delivered.

The questions remain relevant when the components include models and retrieval services. Where does state belong? Which component owns a decision? What evidence makes a result reviewable? Those are the problems I continue to work on.

Cube Cloud is commercial work. This overview describes my role and the platform’s broad technical scope; its source code and internal system details are not public.
